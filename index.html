Instructor notes & the science

What this demonstrates

Treisman and Schmidt studied reports in which features from different objects were recombined. Their colored-letter task directed attention primarily to flanking digits. This adaptation compares a digits-first task with a letters-only task using the same kinds of displays. It uses three different letters and colors, two black digits, a 650 ms fixation, a fixed 200 ms exposure across 32 trials, and a 200 ms pattern mask.

Interpret the results carefully

A recombined pair contains a letter and color that were both shown, but never together. A feature error contains at least one feature that was absent. Correct pairs, partial reports, and blanks are tracked separately. These are descriptive response categories: guesses and memory or response errors can also produce recombined pairs. This demo cannot isolate a perceptual binding mechanism. An individual session may show few or no recombinations, and condition differences are not guaranteed.

This is a teaching adaptation, not an exact replication. Conditions are mixed, reporting uses menus, the mask and display geometry are simplified, and no viewing distance or monitor calibration is enforced. The letters-only condition also changes response demands and retention time. Do not treat its comparison as a controlled estimate of attention alone. The palette is not a color-vision assessment; students who cannot distinguish the colors can observe or discuss the demo instead.

Timing & classroom use

Browser displays are quantized to monitor refresh cycles. The CSV records requested and frame-estimated exposure, not independently measured physical screen timing. Trials interrupted by a hidden tab, window blur, or resize are retried with fresh stimuli. Use fullscreen if helpful; disable distracting notifications. Avoid replaying a trial before responding.

Have students complete the demo before discussing expected errors. Ask whether a reported pair was present, whether its separate features were present, and why an error alone does not establish where in processing it arose. Compare digit accuracy and the number of complete reports alongside recombination rates.

Privacy & sharing

Sessions ended early can be submitted using the results-page button. The page confirms success only after the receiver returns “Saved.” If submission cannot be confirmed, download the CSV and retry or give the file to your instructor. The CSV also includes student details. This page does not use browser storage; reloading clears its local copy. Successfully submitted data remains in the instructor’s Sheet. There are no analytics or external visual assets.
